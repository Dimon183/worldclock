Timezone Information

New York is located in the America/New_York timezone. The offset relative to UTC changes: it is UTC-5 (EST) in winter and UTC-4 (EDT) in summer. The US switches to daylight saving time on the second Sunday of March and back on the first Sunday of November.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time in New York right now?

The exact time is displayed in the large block above. It updates every second.

When do clocks change in New York?

Daylight Saving Time starts on the second Sunday of March (clocks go forward one hour). Winter time returns on the first Sunday of November (clocks go back one hour).
