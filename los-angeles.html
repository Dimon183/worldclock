Timezone Information

Los Angeles is located in the America/Los_Angeles timezone. The offset relative to UTC changes: it is UTC-8 (PST) in winter and UTC-7 (PDT) in summer. The US switches to daylight saving time on the second Sunday of March and back on the first Sunday of November.
