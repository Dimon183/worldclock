What is UTC Time?

Definition: UTC is the primary global standard.

Where used: Aviation, IT, Science.

Diff from GMT: GMT is historical, UTC is atomic.

Why check UTC time?

Meeting at 14:00 UTC would be:

  • 17:00 in Moscow (UTC+3)
  • 15:00 in London (UTC+1 summer / UTC+0 winter)
  • 10:00 in New York (UTC-4 summer / UTC-5 winter)
