What is UTC Time?

Definition: UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the primary global standard for regulating clocks and time. All other time zones are calculated from it.

Where used: In aviation, maritime navigation, IT servers, scientific experiments, and international schedules.

Difference from GMT: GMT is a historical astronomical standard based on Greenwich. UTC is a modern atomic-based standard. For most users, they are identical.

Why check UTC time?

If you work with colleagues abroad or configure servers, knowing exact UTC time is crucial. For example, a meeting at 14:00 UTC would be:

  • 17:00 in Moscow (UTC+3)
  • 15:00 in London (UTC+1 summer / UTC+0 winter)
  • 10:00 in New York (UTC-4 summer / UTC-5 winter)
  • 23:00 in Tokyo (UTC+9)
  • 18:00 in Dubai (UTC+4)
