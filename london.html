Timezone Information

London is located in the Europe/London timezone. The offset relative to UTC changes: it is UTC+0 (GMT) in winter and UTC+1 (BST) in summer. The UK switches to daylight saving time on the second Sunday of March and back at the end of October.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time in London right now?

The exact time is displayed in the large block above. It updates every second.

When do clocks change in London?

Daylight Saving Time starts on the second Sunday of March (clocks go forward one hour). Winter time returns on the last Sunday of October (clocks go back one hour).
