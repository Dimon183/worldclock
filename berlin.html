Timezone Information

Berlin is located in the Europe/Berlin timezone. The offset relative to UTC changes: it is UTC+1 (CET) in winter and UTC+2 (CEST) in summer. Germany switches to daylight saving time on the last Sunday of March and back at the end of October.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time in Berlin right now?

The exact time is displayed in the large block above. It updates every second.

When do clocks change in Berlin?

Daylight Saving Time starts on the last Sunday of March (clocks go forward one hour). Winter time returns on the last Sunday of October (clocks go back one hour).
