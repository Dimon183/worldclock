Timezone Information

Tokyo is located in the Asia/Tokyo timezone (JST). The offset relative to UTC is fixed at UTC+9. Japan does not observe daylight saving time.

Does Tokyo change clocks for daylight saving?

No, Japan has not used daylight saving time since 1952. The time remains constant at UTC+9 throughout the year.
